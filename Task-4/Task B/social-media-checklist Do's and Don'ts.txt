Do's

1) Privacy management: Manage privacy settings to control who sees the posts, photos, and personal information shared.

2) Thoughtful posting: Consider the reflection of the content on the individual's professional image and future job opportunities.

3) Verification of sources: Ensure the authenticity of the information before disseminating it. Only rely on credible sources to avoid the dissemination of false information.

4) Periodic breaks: Take regular breaks from the social media platform. Utilize the app to maintain concentration while studying.

5) Professional use of social media: Utilize the platform for professional development and networking. Use LinkedIn to seek internships and professional advancement opportunities.

6) Prompt reporting of issues: Immediately report grievances such as harassment to the moderators of the platform or the authorities.

Dont's

1) Privacy boundaries: Avoid sharing personal information such as address, phone number, and location in the posts.

2) Constructive behavior: Avoid cyberbullying and negative commenting on others’ posts. Such behavior is not only harmful to others, but it also adversely affects the individual.

3) Information sharing in academics: Avoid sharing detailed academic materials such as examination content and assignment details, as it might lead to charges of cheating.

4) Social comparison: Limit the viewing of others’ highlighting of their lives to avoid unnecessary stress.

5) Emotional posting: Avoid posting content when emotionally charged. Instead, wait for 24 hours before posting to avoid regretting the content posted.

6) Update security: Do not ignore the notification of a potential security breach. Update the password and enable two-factor authentication.
