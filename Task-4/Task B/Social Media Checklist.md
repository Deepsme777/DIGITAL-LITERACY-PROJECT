Social Media Checklist



* Configuring privacy settings to reduce visibility by setting the post to “Friends Only” or “Private.”



* Pre-posting contemplation of the potential impact on individual reputation in the context of higher education and future employment opportunities.



* Avoiding the dissemination of live location information by disabling geotags on stories and reels.



* Authentication of information before dissemination by fact-checking news and linked content to prevent the dissemination of false information.



* Enhancing account security by ensuring the use of robust passwords, enabling 2FA, and ensuring the logout of the account on shared devices.



* Utilizing reporting and blocking options to handle inappropriate behavior, such as addressing harassment through in-app options or the cybercrime helpline (cybercrime.gov.in/1930).



* Regulating digital behavior by setting screen time limits (e.g., setting daily app usage limits) to promote sustained focus on computer science and engineering (CSE) courses.



* Avoiding excessive disclosure of information by not divulging details such as hostel accommodation, examination schedules, or payment identifiers (e.g., UPI IDs).
