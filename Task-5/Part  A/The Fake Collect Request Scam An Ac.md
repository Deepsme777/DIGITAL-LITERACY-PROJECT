##### The Fake Collect Request Scam: An Academic Overview



###### The Nature of the Fraud



The UPI (Unified Payments Interface) fraud refers to the criminal acts perpetrated by malicious individuals who use the popular digital payment system in India for the illicit transfer of money through deceptive means. The "fake collect request scam" is one such form of UPI fraud, whereby the perpetrator tricks the victim into transferring money, believing it to be an incoming payment. This form of UPI fraud has increased in India, with over 1.34 million cases reported in the 2023-2024 period, resulting in overall losses exceeding ₹1,087 crore.





###### The Operational Pattern of the Fraud



* Initial Contact: The scammer makes initial contact with the victim through digital means, such as WhatsApp, SMS, or telephone, where the scammer claims to be the buyer for the product listed on the online platform (e.g., OLX, Facebook Marketplace).



* False Payment Assurance: The scammer agrees on the price quoted for the product, assuring the victim that the payment will be sent immediately using the UPI apps (e.g., PhonePay, Google Pay).



* Collect Request Sent: The victim receives the UPI "collect request," where the victim believes they need to make the payment, while the scammer presses the victim to "approve the request and enter the PIN to receive the money."



* Authorization: The victim, believing the authenticity of the story, clicks on "Pay" and enters the UPI PIN, transferring the money to the scammer's account.



* Disappearance of the Money: The payment ends up as an outgoing payment, where the money does not reflect in the victim's account, and the scammer ends the communication with the victim.
